    Aethermoor a theme park crowd simulation

    At a theme park, every guest is spending one thing they can't get back: time. Aethermoor simulates a full day at a fictional park, with hundreds of guests who each have their own priorities, to see where that time actually goes.

    The question: How much of a guest's day goes to riding, waiting, and walking, and how does that split change as the park gets more crowded?

    Try it: Press play to run the day (9 AM to 9 PM). Use Guests to set how crowded the park is, and Speed to fast-forward. Each pawn is one simulated guest, a small JavaScript program following simple rules about what to ride, when to eat, and how long it'll wait (not an AI). Its color is the guest type, shown in the legend. Drag to orbit the map and scroll to zoom. When the park closes, you get a results summary you can export.

    Work in progress: the charts that break down the time split are being built next.
